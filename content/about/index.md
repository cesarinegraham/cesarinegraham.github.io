I am a graduate student in the [Department of Physics](https://sciences.ucf.edu/physics/) and [Planetary Sciences](https://planets.ucf.edu/) at the [University of Central Florida](https://www.ucf.edu/). 

I earned my B.S. in Astrophysics with a minor in Mathematics from [Michigan State University](https://msu.edu/) in 2024. During my time at MSU, I worked in [Dr. Matthew Schrenk’s Geomicrobiology lab](https://www.mattschrenklab.com/) where I worked on creating more accurate microbial habitability maps and geothermal profiles for icy worlds Europa, Enceladus, Ganymede, and Titan. 

Under the advisement of [Dr. Lauren Mc Keown](https://www.laurenemckeown.com/frostie), I am currently generating geomorphological maps for features on Mars, as well as performing icy analog experiments to characterize the influence of volatiles on places such as Mars, Ceres, Vesta, and possibly the Moon!
